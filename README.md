# 🚀 Forgotten Explorers

**Abandoned, but not forgotten.** A storytelling game where kids meet NASA's retired robotic explorers on the Moon and Mars, help bring them back to life and discover the real science they made possible.

🎮 **Play now:** https://juliatemponibretas-17.github.io/forgotten-explorers/

Built by **StarKeepers** for the **2026 NASA Space Apps Challenge**, challenge *"Abandoned but not Forgotten: Storytelling about NASA's Discarded Equipment on the Moon and Mars"*.

---

## 🌌 About the project

Since the 1960s, NASA has left rovers, landers and probes across the solar system. Kids usually hear only about active missions, so the machines that paved the way are quietly forgotten.

In **Forgotten Explorers**, a little astronaut travels to the Moon and Mars as a *space journalist*. At each stop they find a "resting" explorer, listen to its story in its own voice, help it with a mini-game and take a photo to keep its memory alive.

## 🕹️ How to play

1. Press **Start** and choose your first destination: the **Moon** or **Mars**.
2. **Moon:** meet the Lunar Roving Vehicle (LRV). Jump across craters and dodge rolling rocks to find a lost battery, then ride the rover and visit the Eagle, the descent stage of Apollo 11.
3. **Mars:** meet Sojourner and answer its question about water on Mars. Then tap the passing dust storms, and finally a giant one, to rescue Spirit and Opportunity.
4. **Photos:** drag and zoom the camera to frame each explorer. Your photos go into the final album.
5. **Rocket:** just like Apollo 11's Command Module, your rocket waits in orbit while you explore. Call it back by radio when your mission is done.
6. **Album:** at the end, open the full album with **14 real NASA explorers**, each with a short story based on NASA mission data.

Works on phones, tablets and computers. Available in **English** and **Portuguese**, with sound on/off.
